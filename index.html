About

Alpha Drive One!

ALPHA DRIVE ONE (알파드라이브원), also shortened as ALD1 (알디원), is an 8-member global project boy group under WAKEONE Entertainment. The group was formed through the MNET‘s survival show BOYS II PLANET. The group consists of Leo, Junseo, Arno, Geonwoo, Sangwon, Xinlong, Anxin, and Sanghyeon. They released their first pre-debut single, “Formula”, on December 3, 2025, and officially debuted with the mini album Euphoria on January 12, 2026. The group will promote under a 5-year contract.
